About us

Care, led by people who’ve been at the bedside

Care Collaborative was founded by a nurse and an occupational therapist who saw the same thing again and again: families doing their best to coordinate care with no one to guide them. We built the partner we wished those families had.

Our mission

To help older adults live well, at home, for as long as possible

We combine clinical expertise with genuine advocacy. That means understanding the whole picture, honoring what each person wants for their life, and making sure the care around them actually works together.

We deliberately offer a softer, more human approach: sophisticated and capable, never cold or clinical.

What guides us

  • Advocacy — we work for you and your loved one, first and always.
  • Independence — we protect dignity and choice at every stage.
  • Continuity — one steady point of contact, from the first call onward.
  • Partnership — with families, physicians, and the wider care team.
  • Compassion — because this is personal, and we treat it that way.
