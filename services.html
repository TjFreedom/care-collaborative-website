Our care

How we help

Care Collaborative brings nursing and occupational therapy together under one trusted point of contact, so families get clear guidance and coordinated support instead of a stack of phone numbers.

☉

Care navigation & advocacy

We help you understand the options, coordinate doctors and providers, prepare for appointments, and advocate for your loved one every step of the way.

✚

RN-led nursing support

Registered-nurse oversight of medications, symptoms, and care plans, with a clinical eye that catches concerns early and keeps everyone informed.

☸

Occupational therapy

Practical strategies to maximize independence in daily life, from routines and adaptive tools to maintaining the activities that matter most.

⌂

Aging in place & home safety

Home assessments and plans that reduce fall risk and support safe, confident living at home for as long as possible.

✿

Family collaboration

Shared plans and clear, steady communication that keep everyone aligned and reduce the stress of caregiving.

⇄

Transitions & discharge support

Guidance through hospital stays, rehab, and returning home, so nothing falls through the cracks during the hardest moments.

Not sure where to start?

That’s exactly what we’re here for. Tell us what’s going on and we’ll help you find the right next step.
